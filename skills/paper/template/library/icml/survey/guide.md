# ICML 2026: survey writing guide

Verified: 2026-10-26. Template: icml-2026. Profile: preprint.
This compact guide is for writing; the later format audit must obtain current official sources independently. No claim of submission readiness or conference acceptance.

## Template and presentation

Use [preprint], the icmltitle/twocolumn title block, icmlauthorlist and the supplied surveyAffiliationsAndNotice wrapper (which invokes printAffiliationsAndNotice when affiliation/correspondence information exists). Keep the two-column body, running title and icml2026 bibliography style. Supply author/affiliation commands only when identities were provided; the example authors are not source material.

Use the supplied skeleton, one main.tex and references.bib. Do not edit vendored styles, fonts, margins, spacing or column geometry. A missing user identity stays empty. Preserve readable captions, equations and tables within the column width. Break or reorganize oversized tables rather than shrinking body fonts or margins. Use numbered, referenced floats and meaningful captions; do not invent visual results.

Use title-case content words in titles/headings; keep the abstract in one paragraph (about 4–6 sentences). Citation text retains the body font size. These presentation recommendations are also stated in the official author instructions.

The local preprint wrapper suppresses the upstream missing-corresponding-author placeholder when user identity is absent, while preserving the preprint notice and any supplied affiliation/correspondence information. Keep the protected adapter unchanged.

## Survey writing

Default English, 6,000–9,000 words excluding references, author–year citations with \citet/\citep. Use the conference bibliography style. Abstract summarizes scope, synthesis and limits. Write Introduction, choose Background OR Preliminaries and Taxonomy OR Unified Framework, assemble each approved substantive topic as its own section, then Comparative Analysis and Evaluation, Challenges and Future Directions, Conclusion. Keep stable section labels and dynamic topic names. Paper Agent authors global chapters and preserves Managers' accepted technical meaning.

## Applicable constraints and exclusions

Apply the template's typography, title, citation, figure/table and PDF readability conventions. Submission page caps, double-blind anonymity, deadlines, registration, uploading, commitment and camera-ready publication declarations are outside this preprint profile. They are not format failures. Chinese output is a local language adaptation, not certification of the venue's submission-language policy.

Report documented methods truthfully. Do not invent ethics, conflict-of-interest, funding, permissions or AI-use statements. Read the relevant official policy if a statement is applicable; missing facts stay unresolved. Existing survey limitations belong in comparison/challenges unless an applicable rule requires a separate statement. No mandatory submission checklist is inserted automatically.
